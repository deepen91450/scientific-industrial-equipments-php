INTRODUCTION

 This project is aimed at developing a website that depicts online shopping of scientific and industrial equipments using this software companies can improve their efficiency of their services this website involves all the features of online shopping  firstly customer can browse and select product of his or her interest.  The selected items may be collected in a Shopping Cart then the item in the Shopping Cart will be  presented as an order.  At that time,  more information will be needed to complete  the order.  Usually,  the customer will be asked to fill  or select billing address  and payment information after that a  notification will be sent to customer as soon as order is placed .
There are three roles available:-  
1)  visitor can view available products
2) user can view and purchase the product
3) an Admin has some extra advantages  than user.  Admin can add ,  edit product information and remove products. 
4) Admin can ship order to user based on order sending  mail confirmation.








LIMITATION OF CURRENT SYSTEM

The current system for shopping is to visit 
the shop manually and search for available 
product and choose from it. Sometimes 
products are not available at the shop and 
have to search for products in the other 
shop which makes difficult for user or 
customer to search product. New 
customers don't it know the exact price of 
item and  shopkeeper take advantage of 
this and sell product at high price.





              Advantage of Proposed System

Advantages of my project:
(1) Faster buying process customer can spend less time for shopping for what they want can easily browse through many items at a time and by what they like.

(2)  Flexibility for customers:-customers can order product and services anytime. The result is that seller can offer his item at any place, anytime. There is no limit to see a product.

(3) No reach limitations:- A seller with physical store may only reach a certain number of buyers

(4) More Payment mode:- buyers can pay their orders using UPI, cash on delivery,card on delivery.

(5) If user dislikes any product he/she can return it.
